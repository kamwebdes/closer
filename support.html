Help and support

Quick answers first. If you still need help, send us a message below.

Do I need an account?

No. Closer works without one, and your estimates stay on your phone. An account backs them up, keeps them in sync across devices, and lets you send customers approval links.

How do approval links work?

Open an estimate and tap Send approval link, then text the message to your customer. They see a clean copy of the estimate, type their name, and tap Approve or Decline. Closer marks the estimate won or lost on its own.

I changed an estimate after sending the link.

Open the estimate and tap "Update link with latest version." Your customer sees the new version at the same link.

Why isn't some of my tax showing?

Lines with "Taxable" unchecked aren't taxed. Many states tax materials but not labor. Check your state's rules and set each line to match.

I forgot my password.

In Settings, type your email in the sign-in form and tap "Forgot your password?" We'll email you a link to choose a new one.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Account and backup, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and every estimate in it.
